About Me

I am a motivated and enthusiastic Chemical Engineering student with a strong interest in chemical processes, energy, polymers, and sustainable technologies. I enjoy learning how engineering principles can be applied to solve real-world problems and improve industrial processes.

Alongside my academic studies, I have developed my communication, leadership, teamwork, and problem-solving skills through academic projects, leadership development programs, and community-focused activities. My participation in the Aspire Leaders Program has helped me strengthen my leadership mindset, understand different perspectives, and become more confident in working with people from diverse backgrounds.

I consider myself a curious and hardworking person who is always willing to learn new skills and take on new challenges. I am particularly interested in developing my technical knowledge while also building the professional and interpersonal skills needed to contribute effectively in the engineering field.

My long-term goal is to build a meaningful career where I can combine engineering knowledge, leadership, innovation, and sustainability to create practical solutions and make a positive impact on society.
